ROMANS 6:23
One verse. One conversation. Short, clear, and aimed right at the need of the person in front of you.
One Versenow
For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord. Romans 6:23

How it works

  1. 1
    Need

    Ask, “Is there anything I can be praying for you about?” Then really listen. What they share is the door.

  2. 2
    Transition

    Meet them right where they are, then ask if you can share: “can I share how Jesus can give you peace?”

  3. 3
    Problem

    The wages of sin is death. Sin is choosing to reject God. Wages are what you earn, and sin earns death. It’s why everything feels broken.

  4. 4
    But…

    Pause on the turn. The verse doesn’t end with death.

  5. 5
    Solution

    The gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord. Jesus paid for your sin and mine, so you can be made right with God and know him.

Keep the conversation going

  1. Pray for them, and tell them you are.
  2. Follow up in a few days: “how’s it going?”
  3. Listen for the moment to share the verse.
