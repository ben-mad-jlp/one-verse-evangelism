    The result of sin

    The punishment for sin is death.

    Spirituallynow
    You die inside.
    Physicallynow
    You will eventually die.
    Key takeaway
    1
    Prayer

    Ask how you can pray for them.

    2
    Listening

    Hear the real need in their heart.

    3
    Transition

    Point them to the problem side of Romans 6:23.

    Let’s pray.

    For one friend we can ask this week, “How can I pray for you?”, and for ears to really listen.
